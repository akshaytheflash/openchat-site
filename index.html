Questions

Honest answers.

The app is small on purpose. Here is what it does, and what it doesn’t.

Is OpenChat free?

The app is free. The models are free models on OpenRouter, reached with your own free key. OpenRouter’s free tier allows 20 requests/min and 50 requests/day. After a one-time $10 credit top-up on OpenRouter, that becomes 1,000 requests/day.

What is an OpenRouter key?

A personal API key from OpenRouter, the bridge to dozens of AI models. Every account gets one free. You create it in OpenRouter’s settings and paste it into OpenChat; the app checks it live before saving.

Where is my key stored?

On your device. SecureStore, which is Keychain on iOS and Keystore on Android, plus localStorage on web. The key is sent only to OpenRouter, never to an OpenChat server.

What happens when the free limit runs out?

The app says so in plain language. It is OpenRouter’s free-tier limit, not a paywall. Wait for the window to reset, add credit on OpenRouter for 1,000 free requests/day, or connect another key.

Which models does it use?

By default, openrouter/free, OpenRouter’s Free Models Router, which picks a zero-cost model per message. If the router can’t serve, OpenChat tries individual :free models from the live catalog. You can pin a free model in Settings.

Is there an app?

Yes. It runs on iOS and Android from the same Expo codebase. The Android build is a 94 MB APK — download it here, allow installs from your browser, and you're in. iOS runs through Expo Go until the store build lands. Either way, bring your own key.
